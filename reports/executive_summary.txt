Our latest churn analysis of 5,265 UK e-commerce customers reveals a significant retention vulnerability across your active buyer base. The predictive model identified 1,524 customers who are at high risk of churning, representing 28.9 percent of your total customer population. This vulnerable cohort accounts for 914,695 in historical revenue, averaging roughly 600 in spend per high-risk customer. While these at-risk accounts represent 5.5 percent of your total historical revenue, their loss would severely impact your ongoing sales baseline. Proactive intervention is necessary to stabilize this base before these customers permanently migrate to competitors.

The primary driver of churn risk in your customer base is recency, measured as the number of days elapsed since a customer's last transaction. Customers who do not return quickly face a sharply escalating probability of abandonment, whereas higher purchase frequency and a broader mix of purchased product categories significantly lower this risk. In practical terms, this means your marketing strategy cannot treat disengagement as a slow erosion that can be addressed late in the cycle. Instead, you must view recency as an immediate operational trigger, where inactivity represents an urgent sign of decaying customer habit. Expanding product breadth per customer is equally vital, as multi-category buyers demonstrate far stronger resilience against churn.

To protect this revenue, you should immediately establish automated, recency-triggered win-back campaigns designed to re-engage buyers before they reach the high-risk threshold. You must also build cross-selling programs aimed at broadening the range of products each customer purchases to lock in long-term loyalty. For this targeted re-engagement strategy to be commercially viable, the cost of the incentives and media required to reactivate these customers must remain below the expected lifetime value recovered from the retained group. Furthermore, your win-back offers must achieve a conversion uplift high enough to cover the campaign expenditures across the 1,524 targeted individuals. Focusing intervention on early-stage lapses will deliver the highest return on investment while safeguarding your core customer assets.
